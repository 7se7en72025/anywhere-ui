## How to Contribute

Pick an item from this roadmap, open an issue saying you want to work on it, and follow the guide in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## How to Suggest Something

Open an issue with the `enhancement` label. Describe:

1. What problem it solves
2. Who it helps (developers, users, both)
3. How it fits the thesis (cheap devices, bad networks, other languages, assistive technology)

We优先 items that help the most people in the most challenging conditions.
